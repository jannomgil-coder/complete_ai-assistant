How every prompt is built

  1. RoleTells the AI who to act as, so the answer has the right expertise.
  2. ContextAudience, tone and background, taken from your form.
  3. TaskOne clear job, not several at once.
  4. ConstraintsLength, "do not invent facts" and placeholders for missing details.
  5. Output formatThe exact structure you want back, such as a table of action items.

Use AI responsibly

Check the facts

AI can sound sure and still be wrong. Verify names, dates, numbers and sources before you use anything.

Protect private data

Do not paste passwords, personal details or confidential company information into any AI tool.

Watch for bias

AI learns from human writing, so it can repeat stereotypes. Read the result critically and edit it so it is fair and inclusive.

Each tool has a checklist to tick before you use the result. This assistant builds prompts only. The answers come from the AI tool you choose, and you remain responsible for what you send.
